Privacy Policy

Auxilium Late Attendance Mobile Application
Application: Auxilium Late Attendance (com.auxilium_suite.late_attendance) Last Updated: September 2026

1 Introduction & Institutional Scope

Auxilium Late Attendance is an official institutional administrative utility created exclusively for the authorized faculty, discipline committee members, and campus administrative staff of Auxilium College (Autonomous), Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India.

This application serves exclusively to streamline student late entry verification, replace cumbersome manual ledger entries with fast barcode scanning, and synchronize records with the secure institutional Campus Management ERP.

2 Camera Permission & Hardware Usage

The application requests access to your mobile device's camera (android.permission.CAMERA). The usage of camera hardware is governed by strict privacy safeguards:

  • Local Barcode Decoding: The camera feed is processed strictly in real-time in the device's volatile memory solely to locate and decode 1D/2D student identification barcodes.
  • No Photo Storage: The application does NOT take, save, record, or retain any photographs, gallery images, or video clips on the mobile device.
  • No Third-Party Transmission: The live camera stream never leaves the physical smartphone and is immediately dismissed once the barcode numeric value is read.
Zero Photo Storage Guarantee: Your camera is used purely as an optical optical scanner engine (similar to a handheld laser scanner).

3 Data Collection & Secure Transmission

When an authorized discipline staff member scans a student barcode, only minimal academic identifiers are processed:

  • Student Academic Identifiers: Register Number, Roll Number, or RFID value extracted from the physical student card barcode.
  • Attendance Log Metadata: Timestamp of late entry and the authenticated staff member's system code.
  • HTTPS Encryption in Transit: All data transmissions between the mobile client and the campus servers occur exclusively over industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) encrypted channels.

4 Data Retention & Device Storage Policy

To prevent unauthorized local data extraction or device-loss vulnerabilities:

  • No student attendance records, lists, or histories are permanently stored on the mobile device's internal storage or SD card.
  • The application does not use device tracking IDs, advertising IDs (AAID), or hardware serials.
  • Only the authenticated staff session token and temporary scanning preferences (e.g., sound/vibration toggles) are retained locally in encrypted app preferences.

5 Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

Auxilium Late Attendance is 100% free of commercial tracking:

  • No third-party advertising SDKs (No AdMob, Unity, Meta, or third-party ad networks).
  • No commercial behavioural analytics trackers or user profiling libraries.
  • No sale, lease, or monetization of student or faculty data under any circumstances.

6 Data Rights & Deletion Inquiries

Because attendance records constitute official academic institution records, any requests for verification, record correction, or administrative data review are managed in accordance with institutional statutory policies through the college administration.
