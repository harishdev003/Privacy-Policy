Privacy Policy — Auxilium ERP Suite

Comprehensive user data, device permission, security, and institutional confidentiality standards for students, faculty, and administrative staff.

1

Introduction & Institutional Scope

Auxilium ERP Suite (Package: com.auxilium_suite.harish_selvam) is the official mobile client designed exclusively for the students, faculty members, researchers, and administrative personnel of Auxilium College (Autonomous), Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India.

The application provides a high-performance, secure native shell for the institutional ERP campus portal. It streamlines academic workflows—including attendance tracking, fee receipts, examination results, timetable notices, internal evaluations, and document submissions.

Institutional Autonomy Notice: All campus records, student credentials, and evaluation metrics are administered under the academic statutes of Auxilium College (Autonomous) and affiliated university guidelines.
2

Device Permissions & Transparent Usage

In accordance with Google Play's User Data and Permission policies, the application requests only runtime hardware permissions essential for specific academic features:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly when you intentionally capture photos to upload student profile pictures, document verification attachments, assignment papers, or project documentation inside the portal. The camera is never accessed in the background and no silent photos are ever captured.
  • Storage & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, Photo Picker): Used exclusively when you browse your device files to attach coursework, certificates, or fee vouchers, or when you download hall tickets, mark sheets, and academic circulars to your device.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): Provides an optional on-device app lock for privacy. Handled entirely on-device by the secure Android BiometricPrompt framework. Biometric templates NEVER leave the device's hardware Secure Enclave / TEE and are never transmitted to campus servers.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to establish encrypted HTTPS communication with your campus host server, check connectivity status, and display offline recovery screens during network outages.
3

Data Security, Encryption & Storage

We implement defense-in-depth security standards to guarantee the confidentiality and integrity of your academic data:

  • End-to-End TLS Encryption: All network requests are strictly routed over encrypted HTTPS / TLS 1.3 connections. Plain HTTP transmissions are blocked by default via Android Network Security Configuration.
  • Local Sandbox Storage: Session tokens and user interface preferences are saved in private Android app sandbox storage (SharedPreferences), inaccessible to other apps installed on your device.
  • Android 15 (16 KB Alignment): Compiled with native 16 KB memory page alignment to ensure optimal memory safety, execution efficiency, and long-term operating system support.
Zero Plaintext Logging: No passwords, biometric credentials, or student financial records are written to Android system debug logs (logcat) in release builds.
4

Authoritative Remote Configuration & Maintenance

To ensure 24/7 service transparency, the application queries an authoritative institutional config feed hosted on GitHub Pages (https://harishdev003.github.io/Privacy-Policy/auxiliumsuiteconfig.json).

  • Scheduled Maintenance Broadcasts: During scheduled campus server upgrades, database updates, or term-end migrations, the application automatically displays friendly maintenance banners with estimated completion windows.
  • Dynamic Domain Resolution: Enables institutional network engineers to transition backend server clusters seamlessly without requiring manual Google Play Store updates.
  • Zero Private Tracking: The configuration probe sends no user identifiers, IP tracking beacons, or device telemetry.

Privacy Policy — Auxilium Late Attendance

Administrative barcode scanner utility for campus gate entry discipline and instant attendance record synchronization.

Application Name: Auxilium Late Attendance
Package ID: com.auxilium_suite.late_attendance
Target Audience: Authorized Campus Discipline Staff
Last Updated: September 2026
🔍
1

Introduction & Purpose

Auxilium Late Attendance is an institutional mobile utility built exclusively for the authorized faculty, discipline committee members, and security personnel of Auxilium College (Autonomous).

The application replaces manual paper registers with rapid 1D/2D optical barcode scanning to record late campus entries and synchronize timestamps with the centralized student database in real time.

2

Camera Permission & Optical Scanning Safeguards

The application requires access to device camera hardware (android.permission.CAMERA) subject to strict privacy guardrails:

  • Real-Time Optical Decoding: The camera feed is processed strictly in volatile device memory to read numeric barcode values from physical student identity cards.
  • Zero Photo Storage: The application does NOT take, save, record, or retain any photographs, gallery images, or video clips on the mobile device.
  • No External Media Transmission: Live camera frames never leave the smartphone and are immediately discarded once the barcode is scanned.
Zero Photo Storage Guarantee: Your camera functions strictly as an optical scanner engine (identical to a handheld laser barcode reader).
3

Data Collection & Secure Transmission

Only minimal academic identifiers necessary for student attendance verification are collected:

  • Student Identifiers: Register Number, Roll Number, or Barcode ID from the student ID card.
  • Log Metadata: Exact timestamp of entry and the authorized discipline staff member's identifier.
  • Encrypted Transit: All communications between the mobile scanner and the campus servers occur strictly over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
4

Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

  • Contains no commercial ad networks (No AdMob, Meta, or third-party ad SDKs).
  • Contains no commercial analytics trackers or user profiling libraries.
  • Zero student data sale or monetization under any circumstances.
