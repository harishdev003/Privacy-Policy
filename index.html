Privacy Policy — Auxilium ERP Suite

Official privacy and data governance policy for students, faculty, and administrative personnel accessing the campus enterprise resource portal.

Official Institutional Portal: erp.auxiliumcollege.edu.in ↗
Campus Location: Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India
1

Introduction & Institutional Scope

Auxilium ERP Suite is the official campus mobile application developed exclusively for the students, faculty members, researchers, and administrative staff of Auxilium College (Autonomous), Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India.

The application provides an authenticated, secure gateway to the institutional Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) infrastructure hosted at erp.auxiliumcollege.edu.in. It facilitates legitimate academic workflows including timetable viewing, attendance records, exam hall tickets, mark sheets, fee vouchers, academic circulars, and course material distribution.

2

Information Processed & Usage

The application processes only academic and institutional data necessary to deliver campus services:

  • Academic Profile & Identification: Student register number, department, enrolled courses, attendance percentages, and internal examination marks provided by the college database.
  • Authentication Session Tokens: Secure session cookies and authentication tokens generated upon successful login are kept strictly in encrypted on-device sandbox storage to preserve user sign-in state.
  • Strictly Non-Commercial: Data accessed through the application is used exclusively for educational management and institutional administration.
3

Device Permissions & Safeguards

In strict compliance with Google Play Store User Data and Permission policies, the application requests access only to specific device hardware features when initiated by user action:

  • Camera: Used solely when you voluntarily take photos to upload identity verification documents, project submissions, or profile photos within the ERP portal. The camera is never accessed in the background and no silent photos or videos are captured.
  • Storage & Document Uploads: Used exclusively when you browse your device files to attach coursework, assignment documents, or certificates, or when you download exam hall tickets, receipts, and college notifications.
  • Biometric Authentication: Provides an optional convenience feature enabling you to lock the application with your fingerprint or face recognition. All biometric operations are executed strictly on-device through the Android BiometricPrompt security subsystem. Biometric data is never transmitted over any network and is never stored on college servers.
  • Network & Internet Connectivity: Required to establish encrypted HTTPS communication with the official campus servers at erp.auxiliumcollege.edu.in.
4

Data Security & Encryption in Transit

We apply comprehensive security protocols to safeguard institutional information:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All data transmissions between the application and campus servers are strictly encrypted using industry-standard TLS protocols. Unencrypted HTTP communication is disabled.
  • Local Sandbox Isolation: Session tokens and cache files reside inside Android's private application storage sandbox, inaccessible to other third-party apps on your device.
  • Modern Page-Size & System Compliance: Built and aligned to modern 16 KB page-size standards to ensure optimal memory safety and performance on Android devices.
Protected Credentials: Passwords, payment card numbers, and biometric templates are never stored in plaintext or logged in system traces.
5

Zero Third-Party Advertising & Tracking

To maintain an ad-free, secure learning environment:

🚫 No Commercial Ads
The application contains zero advertising SDKs, banner ads, or commercial promotions.
🛡️ No Data Monetization
Student, parent, and faculty information is never sold, traded, rented, or leased to third parties.
🔒 No Behavioral Trackers
No external commercial analytics or user profiling services are embedded within the application.
6

Data Retention & Institutional Rights

Student educational records and staff employment records constitute official institutional records preserved in accordance with the academic statutes of Auxilium College (Autonomous) and educational governing authorities.

Users can clear their local session cookies and cached pages at any time through the in-app "Clear Portal Cache" option or by resetting app storage in Android device settings.
